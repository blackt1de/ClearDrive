# A4500 Capacity Check — 2026-05-23

Verification pass for the local-only pivot stack: candidates for the A4500 (20GB Ampere) inference box and the 5090 (32GB Blackwell) training box, replacing the current `gemma4:e4b` on Ollama.

## Constraint: 20GB VRAM, target headroom 2GB for system + activations

**Model VRAM formula (used uniformly below):**
- model_vram = total_params × bytes_per_param  (FP16=2, Q8=1, Q4_K_M/AWQ-Int4 ≈ 0.55)
- kv_cache_vram = 2 × num_layers × kv_dim × ctx_len × 2 bytes  (FP16 KV, ctx=2048, kv_dim = num_kv_heads × head_dim)
- total = model + kv_cache + 1 GB activation overhead

For MoE models: model_vram uses TOTAL params (all experts must reside in VRAM), not active.

## Capacity verdict

**Recommended primary: Gemma 4 26B-A4B** at Q4_K_M / AWQ-Int4
- Only candidate that clears the 2GB headroom hard target with comfortable margin (~4.8 GB free at ctx=2048)
- Active-3.8B inference is near-4B-dense speed
- Sliding-window attention (1024) on 24/30 layers keeps real-world KV growth flat past 2K ctx
- Source: https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-4-26B-A4B

**Recommended fallback: Qwen3-30B-A3B** at Q4_K_M
- Fits (17.87 GB) but ONLY ~0.13 GB headroom — any KV growth past ctx=2048 or activation spike → OOM
- Risky as primary; acceptable as text-only fallback if Gemma 4 MoE has tooling issues
- 114 quantized variants published on HF
- Source: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B

**Rejected:**
- **Qwen3-32B (dense)** — Q4 totals 19.35 GB (96.75% of VRAM); fails 2GB headroom. Would need Q3_K_M or lower → quality cliff. Source: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-32B
- **Gemma 4 31B (dense)** — Q4 totals 19.12 GB at ctx=2048; 16 KV-heads × 168 head_dim makes the KV cache the killer. Source: https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-4-31B/blob/main/config.json
- **Gemma 4 27B** — does not exist; use 26B-A4B or 31B. Source: https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-4-27b → HTTP 401

## Recommendations

1. **Primary inference target: Gemma 4 26B-A4B (Q4_K_M or AWQ-Int4) on SGLang main / next release.** Clears every constraint with the most margin.
2. **Fallback inference target: Qwen3-30B-A3B (Q4_K_M) on SGLang ≥ v0.4.6.post1.** Switch to if Gemma 4 MoE has tooling regressions at deploy time. Tight VRAM headroom — pin ctx_len at 2048.
3. **Training: Unsloth ≥ v0.1.35-beta (Gemma 4) / ≥ 2026-02-10 release line (Qwen3 MoE), CUDA 12.8 / torch cu128 / triton ≥3.3.1 on the 5090.** Both candidates work; install is manual but documented.
4. **Spec decoding plan:** Use NEXTN/EAGLE with Gemma 4's `*-assistant` draft on day 1. For Qwen3 fallback, defer spec decoding until a community EAGLE-3 head is verified.
